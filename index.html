About

Namaste, I am Deepak

Gujjar Dhaba started with one simple idea: serve the kind of food we eat at home, to people working and travelling on Kota Road.

My family taught me that a dhaba is judged by its dal and its rotis. So we make both fresh through the day, and we never cut corners on ghee or masala.

Come in for lunch after work at the LIC office, or call ahead and we will keep your meal packed and hot.

  • Family recipes, ground masalas
  • Rotis made fresh in the tandoor
  • Desi ghee in our baati and churma
  • Clean kitchen, clear prices
  • Quick takeaway and WhatsApp orders
